preface: 序 q005R 「遥に劣れめと云」 and 「気候偏倖にして」 unreadable (校記 too); translated literally with a "text corrupt" marker and a note. Please check the facsimile.
preface: 「則是合國なり」 meaning unclear (antipodal "opposite country"?); 西島 unidentified (glossary 疑).
preface: 北極出地九十度 etc.; the 「南北黄道線」 = tropics (note given). 三山六海一平地 proverb glossed from sense only, not verified.
q006L: 《坤輿界全圖》 still unidentified (glossary 疑).
q009L: reading text has 「漢上ニ服屬スル者」; translated as 漢土 with a note (slip?). Check facsimile.
q010L: 鴨緑江 said to be in the 中部 of Korea (error, noted). 兀良哈 given as 兀良哈（朝鲜东北的女真地区）, a gloss of mine; adjust if you prefer.
q012L: 唐東 treated as per glossary note (note given). 「國孫相争テ」: 国孫 translated as "descendants of the ruler" (国主的子孙); unusual word, please check.
q013L: head_zh/head_en carry my gloss for 兀良哈, as above.
q014L: text opens 大宛 (read ダイワン); translated as written with a note.
q016L: 板淡 = "the city is now called Banten": error of the original, noted. 拔答非亞 ruby ターアビヤ → I used Batabiya (glossary), not Tāabiya.
q017L: ruby of 爪哇 here is シヤワ (Shawa); I gave Jawa as elsewhere.
q018L: 其主府 translated as written, note on 王府.
q019L: 又百兒齊亞 (ハルシア): the glossary has no entry for 百兒齊亞 / Harushia; used the form in the note-less bracket. 度兒挌 (hand radical) noted.
q020L: "古ハ印度ニ屬シ" is an error (Armenia never Indian); noted.
q021L: 呂宋 here has ruby ルス → Rusu (Luzon); Hepburn of the kana, glossary has ルス only as "also". ボントハイン: glossary has 邦泰因峰？, I gave Bonthain/Lompobattang in note. ハルカ unidentified as in glossary.
q022L: 前後二大部 = Hither/Further India (note).
q023L: 灵鹫山 / 摩迦陀 identification is an error of the original (not in Ceylon); noted.
q024L: reading_qian.md has 〔舊莫臥兒／スミケイクス。〕 while the glossary and 校記 say 又シケイクス; I followed 校記. 傲虐 vs ruby ダジヤク (惰弱): translated 傲虐, noted. 滿剌甸 = Marathas, romanized Marattten per glossary (probably typo for Marattan).
q025L: 獨逸都 and 赫爾勿亞 are two names (Germany, Switzerland), rubies ドイツ・ヘル/シア as noted in 校記; I took them as two.
q026L: 十七州 vs real eleven provinces noted; 度ミリ → ヨリ noted. 涅垤兒蘭甸/ホルランド/和蘭 heading uses 和兰陀 (the figure label).
q027L: 暎咭唎 vs 英吉利 (エンゲランド): used 暎咭唎 = Britain here, 英吉利 = England; ruby スコシア海 "the sea north of Scotland" per glossary. 五十二州/六十二侯 not checked.
q028R: 「紙數ノ過少ヲ量」 sense odd (過多 expected?); translated literally, noted. English headings: used Qian/Kun for 乾/坤.
