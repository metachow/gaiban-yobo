# tr_en_notes.md — English proofreading, questions for the editor

## 坤

- General (dakuten): the text ruby often omits dakuten that the figure label has (k007L 翁加里亞《ヲンカリア》 vs label ヲンガリア; k009L ホルトカル; k018L ペーリキ …). I have kept the glossary/label romanization (Ongaria, Horutogaru) rather than "Onkaria" etc., as the translator did. Please confirm this is the intended reading of "Hepburn of the kana as written".
- k005L (affects zh): 事ニ臨ミ自ラ危ム者多シ — 危ム is 危ぶむ "to be uneasy, have misgivings", not "endanger oneself". EN corrected to "many of them are full of misgivings when a matter is at hand"; zh 临事而自陷于危险者很多 has the same misreading (suggest 临事多自生疑惧).
- k022L (affects zh): 歐邏巴諸邦東西印度及ビ支那ニ交易シテ至テ富豊ナリ — the US trades with Europe, the East and West Indies and China; it is not "the European states trade with …". EN corrected; zh 欧罗巴诸邦与东西印度及支那进行交易 shares the misreading (suggest 与欧罗巴诸邦、东西印度及支那通商).
- head_en: I changed head_en in k017L (Monomotatsupa → Monomotappa, sokuon as in the glossary); head_en was not listed among my fields, but it is English and the zh proofreader does not touch it. Revert if not wanted.
- 鎮: the translator rendered 鎮 as "garrison" (k011, k012, k014, k018, k021). In this book it stands for an administrative division (Russia's 51 鎮 = guberniyas), so I used "governorate(s)" / "each with its governor". zh keeps 镇, no conflict.
- k020L: the river is 亞瑪鑽《アマツネ》 in the text (Amatsune as written); kept "the Amazone" as the translator did, since it is the same name as the heading. Confirm.
- k011L: 北極地ヲ出ル事西國ニ亘テ — I agree with the translator that 西 must be 兩 ("the two countries"); worth a 校記 check of the facsimile.
- k019L: 亞細亞人此邦ノ人ヲ號シテ長人ト云 — "Asia" is odd (the giant story is European); translated as written, no note. Add a note only if you want one.
- k026R: 船の印本お添 — I keep "add the ships' marks" (船印 = ship flags/insignia); 本 unexplained (perhaps a misreading by KuroNet). 坤 pages k004L, k009L, k024L, k025L: no changes.

## 乾

- preface q005R 氣候偏倖にして正帯の國なり遥に劣れめと云 (kept literal as instructed). Proposal: read 偏倖 = 偏僻 (校記) and the end as 正帯の國に遥に劣れりと云 — "Their climate is extreme, and they are said to be far inferior to the countries of the temperate zone." The present rendering ("whereas the countries of the temperate zone are far inferior") reverses what the argument needs (the temperate 正帯 with Japan and China is the good zone).
- preface: 乾坤二冊 rendered "the two volumes Qian and Kun" (also q028R). The ruby in q028R is 乾《ケン》/坤《コン》; for an English edition that romanizes Japanese readings, "Ken and Kon" would be consistent — or keep Qian/Kun if that is what the edition's volume titles use. Editor's choice; I have not changed it.
- preface: 則是合國なり "This, then, is the united country" kept (note 8 says unclear). 合 may mean "matching / facing" (向かい合う) → "the counterpart country"; no confident reading.
- 北高海 (kind P): I render it "the Caspian Sea" in the text (k012L with note giving *Hokkōkai*; k020L, q009L without). zh keeps 北高海（里海）. If you prefer "the Hokkōkai (the Caspian Sea)" at each first mention, revert k020L/q009L.
- q009L: 韃而靼 — the English now shows the written form ("Dattan (Tartary), written 韃而靼 〔people today omit the character 而〕") so that the small note makes sense.
- q017L note 1 (affects zh): "the Dutch form of the name" removed from EN — アセーム/Asēm is not Dutch (Dutch: Atjeh); it is closer to the older Portuguese/European Achem. zh note 此名为荷兰语式的写法 should be dropped too.
- q019L (affects zh, possible omission): 此國ニ部十五道ニ分ツ — 校記 says the character after 國 looks like ニ, partly broken. Read as 二部 it gives "divided into two parts and fifteen circuits"; both EN and zh omit it ("fifteen circuits"). I left EN unchanged; please decide (if 二部: "The country is divided into two parts and fifteen circuits").
- Glossary typos (romanized column): 滿剌甸 "Marattten" → Maratten; ハルリハキス "Haruriha kisu" → Harurihakisu.
- q028R 紙數ノ過少ヲ量リ: proposal — 過少 = 過多と過少, "weighing the excess and shortfall of leaves", i.e. Europe was split between the volumes to balance their size. That reads naturally and would make note 1 unnecessary (or turn it into "literally 'the excess and shortfall of leaves'"). Not changed, since the note (shared with zh) would have to change.
- q026L 四十九度ミリ五十五度: EN keeps "*miri*" in the text with the note; fine as a literal rendering, but "forty-nine to fifty-five degrees" with the note would read better. Editor's choice.
- q024L: followed 又シケイクス as ruled; romanized Shikeikusu.
