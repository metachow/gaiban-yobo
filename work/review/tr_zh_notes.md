# Questions for the editor: Chinese translation, 坤 volume (proofreader zh)

## 坤 volume (k003L–k026R)

General normalisations (applied throughout the 坤 volume, logged per page):
- 人物 (the people's character) → 其人 / 此地之人, as in the approved k004L ("其人勇壮"); in modern
  Chinese 人物 reads as "figures, characters". Where 人物 means "men of ability" beside 兵威
  (k009L, k010L 人物兵威共盛) → 人才. The 乾 pages still use 人物 throughout; I will normalise them
  the same way unless you rule otherwise.
- 土人 (rendered 土著居民) → 当地人; 往古 → 古时; 歐邏巴 / 亞細亞 (kind P) → 欧洲 / 亚洲 (the
  translator wrote 欧罗巴 / 亚细亚); 亞太臘海 → 亚太腊海（大西洋） at first mention, as in q025L/q027L.
- 窝々 → 窝窝; 斯督汎 → 斯督泛; 小人島 → 小人岛.

Questions / points for the editor:
- k011L 「北極地ヲ出ル事西國ニ亘テ」: 西國 makes no sense; almost certainly 兩國 (the two countries,
  Sweden and Norway) — 西 and 兩 are easily confused. Translated 跨两国计 (the English likewise
  "taking the two countries together"). Please check the facsimile; if the cut has 兩, correct the
  reading text; if it has 西, a 校記 entry would be good.
- k005L note 1 (罗马…皇帝加冕之地，故称“西都”) states a conjecture as fact. 西都 may equally mean the
  capital of the Western Roman Empire, whose heir the German (Holy Roman) emperor claimed to be.
  Suggest softening in both languages ("大概因此称为西都" / "probably hence").
- k005L 「事ニ臨ミ自ラ危ム」 = to feel uneasy (自危), not "endanger oneself": corrected in zh;
  the English proofreader had already corrected the English ("full of misgivings").
- k018L note: besides the Dutch error, the capital of New Granada was Santa Fe de Bogotá, not
  Portobelo (a port on the Isthmus). Add if you want; I left the note as it stands (only made it
  say "此国" like the English).
- k008L 「封内ニ魯西亞獨逸都孛漏生等共和政治ノ處アリ」: kept the translator's reading (a place
  under joint republican government of Russia, Germany, Prussia = Free City of Kraków); zh and en agree.
- k021L 奉行: rendered 长官（奉行） in zh, matching the English "governor (*bugyō*)".
- k026R 「船の印本お添」: kept "附上船的印记"; 印本 might also be "printed specimens"; doubtful as
  the translator said. Not changed.
- k012L 奥蝦夷 (Oku-Ezo = Sakhalin and the Kurils, the northern parts of Ezo): no note in either
  language; a short note would help readers, but would change the note count — your call.

## 乾 volume and preface

- preface q005R 「氣候偏倖にして正帯の國なり遥に劣れめと云」: kept the literal rendering with the
  〔文字有误〕 marker, as ruled. Proposal: read 偏倖 as 偏僻 (as the 校記 suggests) and
  「正帯の國なり遥に劣れめ」 as 「正帯の國より遥に劣れり」 (なり for より, れめ for れり), i.e.
  "their climate is extreme, and they are said to be far inferior to the countries of the temperate
  zone" — 其气候偏僻，据说远不如正带之国 / "Their climate is extreme, and they are said to be far
  inferior to the countries of the temperate zone." This is the only sense that fits the context (the
  author has just described the boiling heat of the warm zone, and his own country is in the
  temperate zone). The present rendering in both languages ("the temperate countries are far
  inferior") says the opposite. If you accept, both zh and en need changing.
- preface: the whole Chinese was semi-classical (…是也, 此间, 云尔, 如覆掌而视); brought to plain
  modern Chinese, sense unchanged. 掌を覆して見るが如し rendered 易如反掌 ("as easy as turning the
  hand"); the English has "as if turning over the palm of the hand" — fine.
- Same normalisations as in 坤: 人物 → 其人 (kept where it means the depicted figures: q006L,
  q007L), 往古 → 古时, 土人 → 当地人, 亞細亞/歐邏巴 → 亚洲/欧洲, 汉土（中国） at first mention per
  page (glossary), 邦人 (reads "Japanese" in Chinese) → 当地人 / 国中.
- Simplified forms in names: the glossary's zh_form keeps some Japanese shinjitai: 亚尔黙尼亚
  (黙 → simplified 默), 麻辣抜儿 (抜 → 拔), 玛泥児诉 (児 → 儿; the text already has 儿), 黒地兀皮亚
  (黒 → 黑; the text already has 黑). I left 黙 and 抜 in q020L/q023L because head_zh (q020L) is
  outside my fields and head and text should agree; please decide, and fix the glossary.
- q019L 「此國ニ部十五道ニ分ツ」: zh and en both translate "divided into fifteen circuits",
  dropping ニ部. Most likely 二部 (kanji 二, indistinguishable from katakana ニ): "divided into two
  parts and fifteen circuits" (cf. k008L 全國八道, k009L 十道…三十部). Not changed (en would need
  the same); please rule.
- q024L note 2: the text says the Sikh state "does not obey the Western countries"; by 1855 it had
  been annexed by Britain (1849). The glossary already has (1799–1849). Suggest adding "1849年为英国
  所并" / "annexed by Britain in 1849" to note 2 in both languages.
- q028R 「紙數ノ過少ヲ量」: 過少 is probably 過不足, "the excess and shortfall (balance) of the
  page count" — i.e. Europe was split between the volumes to balance their size. Proposal: zh
  "考虑到两册页数的多寡", en "weighing the number of pages (so that neither volume is too long or
  too short)", and note 1 could then go or be reduced to a remark. Left literal for now.
- q013L note 3 (the doubled ヲ in 地ヲヲ出ル, "无关文意") is a note on a point that does not affect
  the meaning; the guidelines say such things go in the 校記 only. Suggest deleting in both
  languages.
- q021L: removed the katakana readings shown in brackets after kanji names (望加锡（マカサル）,
  吕宋（ルス）) — the name rule has no place for them. q012L 东京（トンキン，越南北部） kept, since
  东京 alone would be read as Tokyo.
- q026L 「四十九度ミリ五十五度」: zh keeps “ミリ” in the text as the English keeps *miri*;
  alternatively both could print the corrected 至 / "to" with the note (as is done for 天笠 → 天竺).
- q009L: note now cites the new 校記 entry (见校记).
